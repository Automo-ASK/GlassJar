02   The Product

AcaFund — not a payment app.
A financial operating system.

AcaFund manages the entire lifecycle of collective student money: from the moment a class decides to pool funds, to the final expense report that closes the books.

1
PLAN Create a collection with a purpose, amount per member, deadline, and budget allocation breakdown before a single naira is sent.
2
COLLECT A shareable Monnify-powered payment link is generated. Students pay from any bank, any device. No account number. No receipts.
3
VERIFY Monnify webhooks hit AcaFund's backend. Payment is confirmed automatically. Student status flips to PAID. No manual checking.
4
RECONCILE The treasury ledger updates in real time. Every payment is timestamped, referenced, and tied to a member. Disputes die at this step.
5
GOVERN Expense requests go through a role-based approval chain. The Treasurer reviews. The Auditor signs off. Nothing leaves without a trail.
6
SPEND Approved expenses are recorded against the treasury. Every naira is categorised. The balance is always known.
7
REPORT A shareable transparency report is auto-generated. The class sees exactly what was collected, what was spent, and what remains. Trust is not required because proof is automatic.

04   The Story

Meet Aisha.
She didn't sign up for this.

Aisha is the class representative of a 200-level Statistics class. She is a good student, well-liked, and organized. When the class decided to raise ₦1,000,000 for their end-of-year project, they trusted her to handle it.

Nobody told her that "handling it" meant becoming an unpaid bank employee.

Day 1

She sends the collection message

Account details go to WhatsApp. ₦5,000 per student. "Send your receipt." 200 people read the message. 40 people pay in the first hour.

Day 1 — 3 hours later

The screenshots begin

Her phone has 87 new messages. Each one is a payment screenshot in a different format, from a different bank app, with a different reference number. She opens her banking app to cross-check.

Day 2

The disputes start

"I paid yesterday, why is my name not on the list?" She checks again. The money is there. She just missed the transfer in the flood of notifications. She apologises and adds the name manually to Excel.

Day 5

The accountability questions arrive

"How much have we collected?" "Who still hasn't paid?" "What happens to the money if we don't hit the target?" She answers each one individually, from memory, while also attending lectures.

Week 3 — After spending begins

The hardest question

"Aisha, the class contributed ₦1 million. We spent ₦700k on the project. Where is the ₦300k?" She has receipts, but they're scattered across WhatsApp, her notes app, and one PDF on her phone. She spends a weekend reconstructing the record.

With AcaFund

None of this happens

Aisha creates the collection in 60 seconds. Students pay through the link. The dashboard shows who paid and who hasn't. Expenses go through an approval flow. The transparency report answers every question before anyone asks it. Aisha attends her lectures.

AcaFund does not just protect students from a disorganised class rep. It protects a good class rep from a broken system.

06   Governance Model

Accountability is
structural, not personal.

The most dangerous point in student finance is when one person controls both collection and spending. AcaFund replaces personal trust with structural checks.

Role Permissions Key Responsibility
Admin Create collections Manage members Full treasury access Class rep or community leader. Sets up the community, creates collections, and manages membership.
Treasurer Initiate expenses View full ledger Can propose expenses and see all financial records. Cannot approve their own requests.
Auditor Approve expenses Read-only treasury Independent check. Reviews and approves or rejects expense requests. Cannot initiate spending.
Member Pay via link View transparency report Standard student. Pays through the link. Sees the public dashboard and transparency report.

For expenses above a configurable threshold (e.g. ₦50,000), AcaFund requires both Treasurer initiation and Auditor approval before the expense is recorded. This mirrors how real organizations work, applied to the scale of a 200-person university class.

08   Tech Stack

Built to ship fast
and scale further.

The stack is chosen for speed to hackathon demo, clean architecture, and a clear path to production scale.

Frontend
Next.js 14 + TypeScript
App Router for clean layout separation. Server components for fast dashboard loads. TailwindCSS for rapid UI.
Backend
Node.js + Express
REST API with clean route separation for collections, members, payments, expenses, and approvals.
Database
PostgreSQL + Prisma
Relational schema for financial records. Prisma for type-safe queries and fast schema iteration during the hackathon.
Payments
Monnify API
Payment link generation, webhook-based auto-verification. Reserved accounts for persistent collection pools. The actual financial infrastructure.
AI Layer
Claude API (Sonnet 4.6)
Treasury Assistant queries a structured context built from verified ledger data. Grounded AI: no hallucinated balances.
Auth
NextAuth + JWT
Email-based auth with university email validation. Role tokens embedded in JWT for efficient permission checks.
Notifications
Twilio / WhatsApp Cloud API
Payment confirmations, reminder nudges, and expense approval alerts sent where students actually live: WhatsApp.
Hosting
Railway / Vercel
Fast deploys for demo day. Railway for backend and Postgres. Vercel for Next.js frontend. Both free tiers work for hackathon scale.
File Storage
Cloudinary
Expense receipt uploads. Free tier covers hackathon usage. Secure URLs returned and stored against expense records.

The architecture is built so that Monnify is not a bolt-on feature. It is the backbone. Every collection, every verification, every ledger entry flows through it.
